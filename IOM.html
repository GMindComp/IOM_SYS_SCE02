Game Vision & Concept

This experience simulates the journey of a young Egyptian man who travels to Europe and becomes vulnerable to human trafficking. The game aims to build empathy and educate players on the dangers of irregular migration by allowing them to make difficult choices under pressure, directly experiencing how a series of seemingly small decisions can lead down a dangerous path.
